How it works

A simple request flow that keeps everyone on the same page.

Browse

See apartments, the inventory they offer, and the price per day or per week.

Request

Pick dates on a calendar and send a booking request.

Confirm

The request is reviewed, accepted and confirmed with the apartment.

Activate

Your vendor sets up on the booked dates. The Estate Manager sees what is coming.
